Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how Vestend LLC (“Vestend,” “we,” or “us”) handles information on this website, vestend.com. The short version: we do not collect personal information through this website.

Information we collect

This website does not:

  • ask you for any personal information,
  • have forms, accounts, or sign-ups,
  • set cookies, or
  • use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

If you email us

If you write to info@vestend.com, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message. We use this only to reply to you. We do not sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list.

Third-party services

Like all websites, this site depends on a hosting company to work. When your browser loads a page, it connects to the host’s servers, which can see technical details such as your IP address and browser type.

Hosting: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information about visits. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not control GitHub and do not receive personal information from it.

All other files on this site, including fonts and images, are served from this website. We do not load content from any other third party.

Our products

This policy covers this website only. If you buy or use our software, e-learning courses, or ebooks through another store or platform, that platform’s privacy policy also applies.

Children’s privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.

Contact
